I'm a Staff Applied Research Scientist in the Core–LLM group at ServiceNow in Chicago, where I've spent eight years moving from Data Scientist to Senior to Staff. I work across the full loop: designing evaluation benchmarks, fine-tuning models with PEFT/LoRA and RLHF/DPO, and deploying them on vLLM behind Triton Inference Server — closing the loop between model behavior and production outcomes. Before that I did an M.S. in Electrical Engineering at Clemson, with a thesis on detecting and classifying epileptic spikes in patient EEG using HMMs and LPC features. I'm based in Naperville, Illinois, and I'm drawn to work with real product impact and direct contact with the people using it, rather than research insulated from users.
